# Procore Define — marketing sequence

25 seconds: an Eraser overview with a slower handoff into the thirteen-second
Define animation starting at 12s. Open holds until 9.2s, then fades over 3.2s;
the field call and chapter title enter gently over 1.2s, overlapping the fade tail.

0–12.4s: full diagram under “8 Stages of Change Event Workflow”; simultaneous numbered
highlights for Define, Locate, Prepare, Confirm, Execute, Monitor, Modify, Conclude.
Highlights appear together at 1s and hold for four seconds; no bottom label strip.
Locate highlights commitment_id; Execute and Monitor share Pending - In Review.
After a full-map hold, zoom into the original Change Event box, emphasize Define /
Open, hold, and fade into the example. Diagram geometry and labels come from
../../diagrams/procore-feature-dependency.json, rendered transparently at 4× density
to assets/procore-workflow.png. Regenerate from the repository root with:
`node scripts/render-diagram.mjs diagrams/procore-feature-dependency.json videos/eraser-to-problem/assets/procore-workflow.png 4`.

The phone rings and moves left. The manual-entry screen appears, enlarges into a
simplified Change Events / Line Items wireframe, and fills one illustrative row:
Extra concrete; 10 yd³; $150.00 unit cost; $1,500.00 Cost ROM.
The total appears after the inputs. The screen returns to the three-object flow,
which resolves into a Change Event document. The desktop holds one second before
zooming; the document appears promptly after the completed UI shrinks back.
